Spruce · oklch(43% 0.115 168)

Bookbinding cloth. Cross-wheel from the warm paper, so the active book separates cleanly. Low chroma, low lightness — deep rather than bright. Risk: green can read as “done”, which is why status here is grey, never green.

Burgundy · oklch(38% 0.115 15)

Leather binding. Analogous to the paper, so the page reads warmer and quieter overall. Risk: it sits close to --danger, so delete controls have to carry an outline and a word, never colour alone.

Ink only · no accent

Colour has to earn its place. Types read as small caps in ink, the active book is carried by weight and a wash. Nothing is lost functionally. Risk: the type column stops being a column your eye can skip to.

Edit — inspector, not a form

Labels in a 3.6 rem left column, both relationship selects on one row, delete beside save. Four rows instead of nine blocks: about 180 px instead of 460 px.

Why underline, not a lighter box

The border can’t just be softened: WCAG 1.4.11 wants 3:1 on a control’s boundary, and --rule-strong at L 58% is already the lightest that clears it. Dropping three of the four edges keeps that contrast on the edge that remains and removes the weight — and a hairline is what the rest of the page is made of. Toggle Boxed to compare.

Gurney Halleck

He has no relationships, so that part of his row renders nothing — no heading, no “None”, no empty separator. Check it does not read as broken; that rule came from S-03.

Why inputs grow on a phone

30 px tall at 14 px type with a mouse; 36 px at 16 px type under a thumb. Not inconsistency — iOS zooms the whole viewport when a focused input is under 16 px, and S-02 accepted “no zooming” as a criterion. Fine pointers have no such rule, so they get the tighter set.
